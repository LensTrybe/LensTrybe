# Post a Job reel: script

Format: talking head to camera, then cut to the screen video (post-a-job-reel.mp4, 31s) with your voice over it.
Aim for about 45 seconds all up. Speak it like you're telling a mate, not reading an ad.

---

## Part 1: talking head (about 12 to 15 seconds)

> Need a photographer or a videographer?
>
> A wedding, a new listing, a brand shoot, headshots for the team.
>
> You could spend hours scrolling profiles and messaging people just to get a price.
>
> Or you could let them come to you. Here's how easy it is.

*(Cut to the screen video on "Here's how easy it is.")*

## Recording tips

- Record the voice over while watching the video so you land each line on its moment. If you run long, drop "About a minute, that's it." first.
- Record the talking head vertical (9:16), eye level, face in the top half of the frame. The app buttons and captions cover the bottom and right edges.
- Keep the sound effects at full level under your voice. They carry the typing and taps on their own.
- Burn in captions from your edited voice over, so people watching with the sound off still get it.
