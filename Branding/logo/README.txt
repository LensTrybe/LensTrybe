LENSTRYBE LOGO
Option C, editorial caps. Locked 15 September 2026.


WHAT IS IN HERE

svg/        Master files. Use these wherever you can. Infinitely scalable,
            tiny, and the wordmark is outlined so it can never fall back to
            the wrong font on someone else's machine.
png/        Raster exports for anywhere SVG is not accepted.
favicon/    Browser and app icons, plus favicon.ico.


WHICH FILE TO USE

On the dark background          -on-dark
On white or light paper         -on-light
One colour, light background    -mono-ink
One colour, dark background     -mono-white

horizontal  The default. Website headers, email signatures, letterheads,
            anywhere with room to run wide.
stacked     Narrow spaces, square formats, the back of a business card.
mark        On its own, only where LensTrybe is already named on the page,
            or as an avatar or app icon.


THE ONE COLOUR VERSIONS ARE NOT OPTIONAL

A gradient cannot be foil stamped, embossed, engraved, screen printed on a
shirt, or printed by anyone quoting a single colour. Send a printer the
gradient version for those jobs and they will either refuse it or approximate
it badly. Use mono-ink or mono-white.


SMALL SIZES

Everything in favicon/ is the full colour mark on the brand ground. Tested at
true pixel size: it holds comfortably at 32px and up, and even at 16px the pink
ring and mint centre still read, which is more recognisable than a flat version
that small.

The mark on its own, with nothing behind it, is a different matter. Floating on
a background at tiny sizes it loses contrast. That is what the contained dark
ground in the icons is doing, so use those rather than shrinking the bare mark.

Minimum sizes:
  horizontal lockup   160px wide on screen, 30mm wide in print
  stacked lockup      90px wide on screen, 20mm wide in print
  mark alone          24px, on a contained ground rather than floating


CLEAR SPACE

Keep clear space equal to half the height of the lens circle on every side.
Nothing else goes in it: no text, no rules, no other logos, no photo edges.


NEVER

Stretch, squash or rotate it.
Recolour the lens. The gradient is the brand.
Add a drop shadow, glow or outline.
Put the colour version on a busy photograph. Use mono-white.
Re-typeset the wordmark. The tracking is part of the logo, not a suggestion.
Use the wordmark without the mark on anything external.
